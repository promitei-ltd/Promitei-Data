About TechGuard Labs Foundation

TechGuard Labs Foundation is the Israel–India Cybersecurity Innovation Centre in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. It was founded by Israeli and Indian partners, including SNR Sons Charitable Trust, Ariel University and Promitei Ltd., to bring Israeli cybersecurity consulting services and technologies to South India.

The team of 30+ people works with exchange-listed companies, major manufacturers and leading hospital groups in Coimbatore. Israeli experts travel to Coimbatore regularly to work on site with local companies. TechGuard took part in Israel Cyber Day in May 2026.

Advisory and compliance
Readiness assessments, regulatory gap analysis and compliance roadmaps aligned with the DPDP Act, ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR.
Security testing and validation
Penetration testing, configuration and posture reviews, and control effectiveness testing.
Security architecture
Security design reviews for cloud, on-premises and hybrid environments: Zero Trust segmentation, identity architecture (MFA, SSO, least privilege) and hardening baselines.
Managed security services
SOC/NOC-aligned monitoring, 24/7 incident monitoring, triage and escalation, vCISO services and managed vulnerability and patch governance.
Healthcare data protection

Clinical data anonymisation
A TechGuard project anonymises and processes hospital data, preparing clinical records for use in pharmaceutical and biotech research.

Why an in-house cybersecurity team matters

Hospitals share data only when they trust how it is handled, and buyers need the same assurance. Having cybersecurity specialists inside the data pipeline gives both.

01

Extraction inside the hospital

Security specialists can run extraction and de-identification on hospital premises, so identifiable records stay inside the hospital and only de-identified data moves to the dataset.

02

De-identification tested for leaks

A security team can design de-identification and then test it as an attacker would, checking free text and rare combinations of dates, places or diagnoses for re-identification risk.

03

Controlled access, audit trail

TechGuard's healthcare service covers least-privilege access audits and audit-ready logging. Applied to the data pipeline, every access to patient data can be traced and reviewed.

04

Private processing environment

The AI data centre provides a private cloud and in-house AI models, so datasets are prepared and analysed on infrastructure the partners control.

05

Faster client security reviews

Buyers' security and privacy questionnaires go to an in-house cybersecurity team that knows the controls first-hand, which can shorten due diligence before a data project starts.

06

DPDP Act and incident readiness

TechGuard's healthcare work is aligned with the DPDP Act, HIPAA and CERT-In. Its 24/7 incident monitoring and breach notification playbooks support a fast, documented response.
