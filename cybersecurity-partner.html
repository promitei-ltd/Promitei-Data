About TechGuard Labs Foundation

TechGuard Labs Foundation is the Israel–India Cybersecurity Innovation Centre in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. It was founded by Israeli and Indian partners, including SNR Sons Charitable Trust, Ariel University and Promitei Ltd., to bring Israeli cybersecurity expertise and technology to South India.

Advisory and compliance
Readiness assessments, regulatory gap analysis and compliance roadmaps aligned with the DPDP Act, ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR.
Security testing and validation
Penetration testing, configuration and posture reviews, and control effectiveness testing.
Security architecture
Security design reviews for cloud, on-premises and hybrid environments: Zero Trust segmentation, identity architecture (MFA, SSO, least privilege) and hardening baselines.
Managed security services
SOC/NOC-aligned monitoring, 24/7 incident monitoring, triage and escalation, vCISO services and managed vulnerability and patch governance.
Healthcare data protection
Mapping every system that touches patient data, least-privilege access audits, audit-ready logging, medical device (IoMT) assessments and ransomware readiness.
Clinical data anonymisation
Anonymisation and processing of hospital data for pharmaceutical and biotech research.

Why an in-house cybersecurity team matters

Hospitals and data buyers both need proof that patient data is handled securely. Security specialists inside the data pipeline provide it.

01

Extraction inside the hospital

Extraction and de-identification run on hospital premises. Only de-identified data leaves the hospital.

02

De-identification tested for leaks

Security specialists test de-identification the way an attacker would, including free text and rare combinations of dates, places and diagnoses.

03

Controlled access, audit trail

Least-privilege access and audit-ready logging make every access to patient data traceable.

04

Private processing environment

Datasets are prepared and analysed on infrastructure under our control: the AI data centre's private cloud and in-house AI models.

05

Faster client security reviews

Your security and privacy questionnaires go straight to the team that runs the controls, which shortens due diligence.

06

DPDP Act and incident readiness

Work aligned with the DPDP Act, HIPAA and CERT-In, backed by 24/7 incident monitoring and breach notification playbooks.
