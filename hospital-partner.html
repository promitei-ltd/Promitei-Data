About the hospital

Sri Ramakrishna Hospital (SRH) has served Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, since 1975. Established and run by the SNR Sons Charitable Trust, it is a 950+ bed multispeciality hospital on an 18-acre campus in the heart of the city. It has a new 230-bed super speciality block and 1,400+ qualified staff, and has treated more than one million patients.

SRH has been NABH accredited since 2017 and holds NABH Digital Health accreditation at Gold Level and NABL accreditation for medical laboratories. It has trained doctors in NBEMS postgraduate programmes since 1995, and its registered Ethics Committee reviews Phase 3 and 4 trials and observational studies.

Why its data is deep

The whole patient journey in one institution
Emergency, intensive care, surgery, oncology, transplantation and rehabilitation run across 44 centres of one hospital, so diagnosis, treatment and follow-up are documented within the same institution.
Diagnostics produced in-house
Core imaging, laboratory and pathology run in-house: 3T MRI, dual-source CT, PET-CT, an NABL-accredited laboratory, and histopathology with 100+ IHC markers and HER2 dual ISH.
Coded, digital records
Diagnoses and procedures are coded to ICD-10 and ICD-9-CM, paper records are digitised, and EHR, LIS and RIS systems are in use; SRH holds NABH Digital Health Gold accreditation.
Ethics Committee and long-term follow-up
A teaching hospital since 1995 with a CDSCO- and DHR-registered Ethics Committee, and continuous care since 1975, including congenital heart patients followed into adulthood in a dedicated GUCH clinic.
