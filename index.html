Our data

Core data types across outpatient, inpatient, diagnostic and treatment care, combined and scoped to your brief.

Clinical Records

Structured and narrative records across outpatient and inpatient care.

  • Demographics and ICD-10 diagnoses
  • Consultations and discharge summaries
  • Medications and treatment history
  • Follow-up and outcomes

Medical Imaging

Diagnostic imaging with radiologist reports.

  • CT, MRI and X-ray
  • PET-CT, mammography, ultrasound
  • Echocardiography and angiography
  • DICOM with standardised metadata

Pathology & Cytology

Histopathology results with a physical tissue archive.

  • H&E and IHC reports
  • Cytology and frozen section
  • Tissue blocks and slides
  • Slide digitisation on request

Laboratory Diagnostics

Structured test results tracked over time.

  • Haematology and biochemistry
  • Hormones and tumour markers
  • Microbiology and sensitivity
  • Genetic test reports

Signals & Functional Tests

Physiological recordings and functional assessments.

  • ECG, Holter, treadmill test
  • EEG, EMG and nerve conduction
  • Spirometry and audiometry
  • ICU monitoring charts

Procedures & Treatment

What was done to the patient and what followed.

  • Surgical and interventional procedures
  • Chemotherapy, radiotherapy, dialysis
  • Inpatient medications
  • Complications and readmissions
