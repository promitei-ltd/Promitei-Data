Who we are

Promitei Ltd. is based in Israel and supports the path from research to commercial product to growing markets. Technology transfer is its core competence.

In India, Promitei Ltd. co-founded TechGuard Labs Foundation, the Israel–India Cybersecurity Innovation Centre in Coimbatore. The company's long-standing cooperation with Ariel University covers life sciences and cybersecurity education. Promitei Data is its medical data venture for clinical research.
